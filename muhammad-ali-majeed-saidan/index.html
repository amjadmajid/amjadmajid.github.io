01 / About

A clinical foundation.
A research perspective.

Dr Muhammad Ali Majeed-Saidan is a paediatrician and clinical researcher whose published work spans neonatal medicine, congenital anomalies and infant outcomes.

His professional record includes neonatal intensive care at Riyadh Military Hospital and research in the Division of Neonatal Medicine at Prince Sultan Military Medical City, Riyadh. His work connects clinical observation with studies of newborn screening, birth-defect risk factors and early-life survival.

A recurring theme across this research is the value of understanding what happens before, at and after birth—and using that evidence to inform care for newborns and families.

Research in focus / 2019

Following newborn health
beyond birth.

A prospective study at Prince Sultan Military Medical City examined congenital heart defects, associated risk factors and survival. Recruitment covered births from July 2010 to June 2013, with follow-up to two years of age.

Dr Majeed-Saidan was the first and corresponding author of the resulting 2019 publication in the Journal of Congenital Cardiology.
