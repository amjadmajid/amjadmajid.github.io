01 / About

A clinical foundation.
A research perspective.

Dr Muhammad Ali Majeed-Saidan is a paediatrician, neonatologist and clinical researcher whose career spans Iraq, the United Kingdom and Saudi Arabia. He served as Consultant Neonatologist and Head of the Division of Neonatal Medicine at Prince Sultan Military Medical City, Riyadh, from 1994 to February 2012.

A medical graduate of the University of Baghdad, he gained MRCP (UK) in paediatrics in 1981 and became a Fellow of the Royal Colleges of Physicians of London and Edinburgh in 1995. His early research examined methylmercury poisoning in children and infants following the 1971–1972 outbreak in Iraq, including work with the World Health Organization.

His later work encompasses neonatal outcomes, congenital anomalies and birth-defect surveillance. Alongside research, he has taught clinical trainees, served on the editorial board of the Saudi Medical Journal, and written two Arabic books reflecting on medical practice.

Research in focus / 2019

Following newborn health
beyond birth.

A prospective study at Prince Sultan Military Medical City examined congenital heart defects, associated risk factors and survival. Recruitment covered births from July 2010 to June 2013, with follow-up to two years of age.

Dr Majeed-Saidan was the first and corresponding author of the resulting 2019 publication in the Journal of Congenital Cardiology.
