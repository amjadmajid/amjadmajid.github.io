## Research, education and service

The three website research areas are editorial groupings: **neonatal care and outcomes**, **congenital anomalies and risk factors**, and **screening and birth-defect surveillance**. They draw on studies of neonatal survival, congenital hypothyroidism, congenital heart and central nervous system anomalies, consanguinity, folic-acid use, and registry development. They are not formal appointments or claimed programme titles.

The featured 2019 heart-defects study included **28,646 eligible births**, recruited from July 2010 through June 2013, with follow-up to age two. This is the study cohort, not a count of patients personally treated. Its publication date is 20 March 2019; recruitment dates must not be presented as publication dates. See the [publisher article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40949-019-0023-8).

He coauthored the **2005 Bubble nasal CPAP manual** for the Riyadh Al-Kharj Hospital Programme. Its title page identifies it as an adaptation of a prior training manual by J. M. Conner, R. de Klerk and A. de Klerk. The [Fiocruz-hosted manual](https://portaldeboaspraticas.iff.fiocruz.br/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/3-AMMARI-Amer-KASHLAN-Fawaz-EZZEDEEN-Faisal-AL-ZAHRANI-Atyah-KAWAS-John-MAJEED-SAIDAN-M.A..pdf) supports an educational contribution; it is linked as a historical resource, not current clinical guidance.

He coauthored the [2015 national birth-defect registry editorial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25719575/). A [publisher acknowledgement](https://journals.lnh.edu.pk/lnjpc/pdf/33e8111c-db81-48f1-b142-a1425d4b9da7.PDF) names him among reviewers for the **2024** volume of *Liaquat National Journal of Primary Care*. ## Profiles, confirmation needs and editorial choices

Verified or located professional links include [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1705-7540), [Kudos](https://www.growkudos.com/profile/muhammad_ali_majeed-saidan), and a [LinkedIn entry](https://sa.linkedin.com/in/muhammad-ali-majeed-saidan-89272b71). LinkedIn’s indexed name and institutional association were visible, but its detailed/current status was not verified. ORCID did not expose a usable works list. The supplied Scholar URL is preserved with these limitations.

**Owner confirmation remains needed** for a current appointment, current clinical practice, university titles, education dates, awards, full CV, preferred contact information and an approved portrait. The 2019 corresponding-author section publicly lists **msaidan@hotmail.com**. This is a historical professional correspondence address for owner confirmation; it has not been added to the public site. No current telephone or address is inferred.

A [Prabook biography](https://prabook.com/web/muhammad_ali.majeed-saidan/549606) suggests Baghdad medical education in 1969, Baghdad posts in 1971–1975, Diwaniyah in 1976–1977, London posts in 1978–1985, and Riyadh work from 1985. **These are unverified secondary leads, not approved website facts.** Its 1975 qualification wording appears unreliable; its Who’s Who mention is not treated as an award.

The site therefore uses a professional research focus, dated contributions, source notes, and original decorative artwork instead of an unverified portrait. It avoids current-practice advertising, invented achievements, citation metrics, unsupported professorships and a supposed complete publication count. Research summaries are paraphrases, topic filters are editorial classifications, and original records remain accessible through links. Future additions should retain the same evidence standard and distinguish source dates from present-day status.
