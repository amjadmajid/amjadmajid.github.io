## Research, education and service

The four website research areas are editorial groupings: **neonatal care and outcomes**, **congenital anomalies and risk factors**, **screening and birth-defect surveillance**, and **environmental toxicology and child health**. They draw on studies of neonatal survival, congenital hypothyroidism, congenital heart and central nervous system anomalies, consanguinity, folic-acid use, and registry development. They are not formal appointments or claimed programme titles.

The featured 2019 heart-defects study included **28,646 eligible births**, recruited from July 2010 through June 2013, with follow-up to age two. This is the study cohort, not a count of patients personally treated. Its publication date is 20 March 2019; recruitment dates must not be presented as publication dates. See the [publisher article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40949-019-0023-8).

He coauthored the **2005 Bubble nasal CPAP manual** for the Riyadh Al-Kharj Hospital Programme. Its title page identifies it as an adaptation of a prior training manual by J. M. Conner, R. de Klerk and A. de Klerk. The [Fiocruz-hosted manual](https://portaldeboaspraticas.iff.fiocruz.br/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/3-AMMARI-Amer-KASHLAN-Fawaz-EZZEDEEN-Faisal-AL-ZAHRANI-Atyah-KAWAS-John-MAJEED-SAIDAN-M.A..pdf) supports an educational contribution; it is linked as a historical resource, not current clinical guidance.

He coauthored the [2015 national birth-defect registry editorial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25719575/). A [publisher acknowledgement](https://journals.lnh.edu.pk/lnjpc/pdf/33e8111c-db81-48f1-b142-a1425d4b9da7.PDF) names him among reviewers for the **2024** volume of *Liaquat National Journal of Primary Care*. The acknowledgement was verified through indexed publisher text; direct retrieval failed. It does not establish editorial-board membership.

## Early author identity and bibliography corrections

The CV's research narrative and publications explicitly connect **M. A. Majeed** to Muhammad Ali Majeed-Saidan. The three WHO outbreak papers use **M. A. Majid**, a form also present in the CV's references. Historical indexed names are preserved, and only the specifically sourced records are merged. A name match alone is insufficient for adding other Majeed/Majid papers.

The collective Clinical Committee Report, conference abstracts, secondary abstract/chapter versions and duplicate Scholar-style entries are not counted as additional journal papers.
