02 / Clinical & research appointments

A career across three countries.

From early research on environmental exposures to neonatal intensive care, congenital-anomaly studies and birth-defect surveillance.

  1. July 1972 – September 1973

    Research Fellow, World Health Organization

    Epidemiological assessment of the 1971–1972 methylmercury poisoning outbreak in Iraq. His subsequent publications examined exposure and outcomes in children, infants and mothers.

  2. 1973–1977

    Paediatrics in Iraq

    Senior Resident in Paediatrics at Medical City Teaching Hospital, Baghdad (September 1973–September 1975); Consultant Paediatrician at Diwanyia Republic Hospital (October 1975–November 1976); and Senior Registrar in Paediatrics at Medical City Teaching Hospital (November 1976–April 1977).

  3. 1977–1985

    Research and clinical development

    A Mercury Research Project fellowship between Baghdad and Rochester, New York (July 1977–February 1978), and paediatric and neonatal training and clinical appointments in the United Kingdom. These included Hammersmith, Queen Mary’s and St Stephen’s hospitals, followed by locum consultant work in London.

  4. September 1985 – January 1988

    Senior Registrar in Paediatrics

    Riyadh Armed Forces Hospital, Saudi Arabia.

  5. February 1988 – 1994

    Consultant Paediatrician

    Riyadh Armed Forces Hospital, with a special interest in neonatal medicine.

  6. 1994 – February 2012

    Consultant Neonatologist & Division Head

    Head of the Division of Neonatal Medicine at Prince Sultan Military Medical City, Riyadh. His clinical work encompassed intensive care for premature and term newborns, alongside research and postgraduate teaching.

  7. February 2012 – September 2014

    Deputy Principal Investigator

    “Pattern of Fetal Malformations in a Saudi Population,” a cohort research project examining congenital anomalies and associated risk factors.

  8. September 2014 – June 2015

    Birth-defect registry adviser

    Adviser to the Medical Services Department Birth Defect Registry (MSD-BDR). The ICBDSR annual reports also document an Associate Director role in 2013–2014.

03 / Teaching & professional service

Sharing experience.

Clinical education

Teaching across career stages

Teaching nurses, medical students and postgraduate trainees in Iraq, the United Kingdom and Saudi Arabia. Clinical Tutor at Riyadh Armed Forces Hospital (1987–1989), and faculty and organising-committee member for the MRCP / Arab Board revision course (1988–1994).

Editorial work

Contributing to medical publishing

Editorial Board member of the Saudi Medical Journal, 2003–2008. Later professional service includes acknowledgement as a reviewer for the Liaquat National Journal of Primary Care in 2024.

Scientific meetings

Bringing clinicians together

Scientific Committee Chairman of the First Saudi Neonatal Neurology Symposium in Riyadh, 13–15 March 2011. His CV also records scientific and organising-committee contributions to paediatric and neonatal meetings between 1987 and 2013.

Recognition

Service to neonatal medicine

2007: Recognition from the Department of Paediatrics, King Faisal Specialist Hospital and Research Centre, Riyadh, for twenty years of service in neonatal medicine in Saudi Arabia.

2014: Recognition from the Department of Paediatrics, King Fahad Armed Forces Hospital, Jeddah, for contributions to its Hot Topics in Neonatal Medicine symposium.
